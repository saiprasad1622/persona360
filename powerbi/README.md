# Persona360 Power BI Dashboard

## Data Source

customer_360.csv

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – Executive Overview

- Total Customers
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- High Churn-Risk Customers

Visuals:

- Revenue by Persona
- Revenue by City
- Customers by Plan
- Churn Risk Distribution

### Page 2 – Customer Personas

Visuals:

- Customer Count by Persona
- Revenue by Persona
- Engagement Score by Persona
- Persona by Customer Type

### Page 3 – Customer Engagement

Visuals:

- Campaign Clicks
- Campaign Opens
- Web Events
- Average Web Session Duration
- Engagement Score

### Page 4 – Support & Churn

Visuals:

- Support Tickets
- Open Tickets
- Average Resolution Hours
- Churn Risk
